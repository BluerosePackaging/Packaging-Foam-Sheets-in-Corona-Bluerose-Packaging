## Which Foam Sheet Is Right for Your Product?

The material should match the job rather than simply the size of the package. BlueRose Packaging offers different foam sheet options for a range of protective packaging applications.

* **Polyethylene foam sheets:** A closed-cell option often used when products need cushioning and a firmer protective layer.
* **Polyurethane foam sheets:** A flexible option that can help cushion delicate items and fill spaces within packaging.
* **Anti-static foam sheets:** Worth considering for electronic components that require protection against electrostatic discharge.
* **Custom foam sheet options:** Useful when standard sheet dimensions or configurations do not suit the product being packed.

Before choosing a material, consider the product's weight, surface finish, fragility, and how it will be transported. For electronics, verify that the selected foam is appropriate for the item's electrostatic protection requirements.

## Where Packaging Foam Sheets Can Be Used

Foam sheets serve different purposes across manufacturing, distribution, and fulfillment operations. Their role depends on the item and the way it is packed.

**Electronics and electrical components:** Sheets can help separate housings, accessories, and selected components. Static-sensitive parts may require anti-static materials.

**Furniture and decorative products:** Protective layers can be placed between finished panels or around surfaces that could be marked during transit.

**Industrial equipment and spare parts:** Foam can separate metal or plastic components and reduce surface-to-surface contact inside a shipping carton.

**Glass, ceramic, and delicate goods:** When paired with suitable outer packaging, foam sheets can provide an additional cushioning layer around fragile items.

**Retail and e-commerce fulfillment:** Packing teams can use foam between products or to cover selected surfaces before closing the shipping box.
